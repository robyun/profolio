Before my Ph.D. at GT, I worked on experimental neuroscience, data analysis, and embedding system.

## Tactile processing and experimental tools

**Favorov Lab · UNC-Chapel Hill**  
Research assistant with Oleg Favorov · February 2024-May 2026

- I studied tactile processing in functional modules of rat somatosensory cortex with multisite NeuroNexus probes. I extracted spikes from raw recordings, clustered units, and helped with acute and chronic implantations.

- I helped assess transcranial focused ultrasound safety with histology of stimulated cortical tissue. I also built a YOLO model to track rat paws in live infrared video and return bounding boxes and paw features.

- I designed MRI tests for millimeter-scale rare-earth fiducials and built behavioral setups with Arduino, CAD, laser cutting, and 3D printing.

## Neural activity during decision-making

**Wallis Group · UC Berkeley**  
Research assistant with Joni Wallis · May-September 2025

I studied decision-making in the primate hippocampal-prefrontal circuit. I wrote MATLAB software to synchronize MonkeyLogic behavioral data with Plexon spike recordings, plot spike density, and run PCA and regression. I used the Hilbert transform to measure cross-trial phase alignment in local field potentials.

## Embedded sensing for joint motion

**Biomedical engineering senior design capstone**  
Team lead and electronics lead · August 2025-May 2026

I built a prototype with two MPU9250 IMUs, quaternions, and Madgwick filtering to track relative joint angles in real time. It included sensor calibration, startup zeroing, Euler-angle conversion, and alerts at motion thresholds. I wrote Python tools to receive the sensor data and plot angle changes live.

## Earlier research

**Magnon-photon coupling · Zhang Research Group, UNC-Chapel Hill**  
Research assistant with Wei Zhang · February 2023-January 2024

I designed circuit hardware with Fusion 360 and CNC milling for gain-driven polariton experiments and studied control of magnon-photon interactions. The work contributed to a [2024 paper in Physical Review Applied](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevApplied.21.034034).

**Functional connectivity · Yale School of Medicine**  
Research assistant with Chiang-Shan R. Li · September 2022-January 2023

I used MATLAB statistical tools to study how drinking severity relates to resting-state hypothalamic connectivity. Our [2024 QIMS paper](https://doi.org/10.21037/qims-24-815) examined links to behavioral traits and differences between women and men.
